### Overview

Sara serves as the visual avatar for AI interactions, providing users with a clear indication when they are engaging with artificial intelligence. Use Sara to signal direct interactions with an AI agent, such as during text or voice conversations with AI.

### Usage Guidelines

#### When to Use Sara

- **Direct AI Interactions**: Use Sara where users interact directly with the AI agent, such as through chat or voice conversations
- **AI Agent Representation**: When you need to represent the AI as a conversational entity
- **AI Presence Indication**: To clearly signal when AI is actively engaged in the interaction

#### Sara as Agent

Use Sara where users interact directly with the AI agent, such as through chat or voice conversations. This helps establish a clear understanding that users are communicating with an artificial intelligence system.

#### States Usage

- **Default State**: Use for standard AI interactions and when the AI is actively engaged
- **Resting State**: Use when the AI is in a passive or idle state, providing visual feedback about the AI's current status

#### Size Guidelines

- **32px (Regular)**: Use for compact interfaces and secondary AI indicators
- **48px (Medium)**: Use for standard UI elements where Sara needs moderate prominence
- **64px (Large)**: Use for primary AI interactions and when Sara needs high visibility
- **Custom Sizes**: Use larger custom sizes for hero sections or when Sara is the primary focus of the interface
