### Overview

AI Chip provides a compact way to represent AI-powered actions in a contextual and dynamic manner. Built on the foundation of Action Chips, it features distinctive visual indicators that clearly communicate AI functionality to users.

<RenderBlock of={AIChipStories.All} />

### States

AI Chip supports **5 states** - default, hover, active, focus and disabled.

### Usage Guidelines

#### When to Use AI Chip

- **AI-Assisted Actions**: Use for actions that are powered or assisted by AI capabilities
- **Contextual AI Features**: When you need to indicate AI functionality within a specific context or workflow
- **Dynamic Actions**: For AI actions that may change based on content or user behavior
- **Space-Efficient AI Indicators**: When you need to conserve space while clearly indicating AI functionality

#### AI Chip for AI Assisted Actions

AI Chip should be used where there are actions that are assisted or powered by AI. The distinctive appearance helps users identify which actions leverage AI capabilities, setting appropriate expectations for the experience.

#### Best Practices

- **Clear Labeling**: Use descriptive labels that clearly indicate the AI action being performed
- **Appropriate Icons**: Choose icons that reinforce the action and are universally understood
- **Consistent Usage**: Apply AI Chips consistently across similar AI-powered features in your application
- **Contextual Placement**: Position AI Chips where users would naturally expect to find the related functionality
